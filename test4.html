Selection & Workforce Portal Overview

Welcome to our integrated recruitment and payroll management system. Access technical assessments and manage active employee attendance seamlessly.

Portal Features & Instructions

  • Attendance Register (Tab 2): Track 30-day employee attendance records (P = Present, A = Absent, OD = On Duty, HD = Half Day, SL = Short Leave). Includes Excel Export.
  • Payroll Calculation (Tab 3): Dynamically calculates payable salaries based on attendance inputs from Tab 2. Includes Summary Cards, Table Grand Totals, and Excel Export.
  • Technical Assessments (Tabs 4-8): Contains real questions dynamically loaded from your Excel sheets with automatic saving and Word Document (.docx) Export.
  • Submission Modal: Upon completing the final slide in any test, click Submit Paper to open the review status pop-up.
